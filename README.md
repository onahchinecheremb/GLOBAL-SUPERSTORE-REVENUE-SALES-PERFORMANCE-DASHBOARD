# 📊 Global Superstore Revenue & Sales Performance Dashboard

## Introduction and Overview

*The global superstore Revenue and Sales performance dataset*  is a dataset that is generated from a retail superstores across states in United state.
This project analyzes four years (2015–2018) of the Global Superstore dataset 
using Excel, SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS) and Microsoft Power BI. 

The objective is to identify the top performing product categories, regions, customer segments, and products by revenue while providing an interactive dashboard that supports strategic business decision making.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales performance, identify revenue drivers, evaluate customer behavior, and explore business trends through interactive visualizations.

## 📂 Data Description

The Global Superstore dataset contains historical sales transactions from 2015 to 2018. It includes information on customers, products, sales, shipping, and geographic locations.

Key Attributes

* Order Date
* Ship Date
* Customer ID
* Customer Name
* Segment

* State

* Category
* Sub-Category

The dataset was prepared and validated before analysis to ensure consistency and accuracy.

## 🔍 Insights

The analysis revealed several key business insights:

* Technology generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
* The West Region recorded the highest sales performance.
* Consumer customers contributed the largest share of revenue.
* Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
* Revenue showed consistent growth throughout the analysis period.

These insights provide management with a clearer understanding of where revenue is generated and where strategic efforts can be focused to maximize business performance.

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Invest in High Performing Product Categories

Increase inventory availability and marketing investment in product categories that consistently generate high revenue to maximize sales opportunities.

- Strengthen High Revenue Regions

Allocate additional operational and marketing resources to regions with strong sales performance while maintaining adequate inventory levels.

- Improve Performance in Underperforming Regions

Conduct further market analysis to understand the factors affecting lower performing regions and implement targeted promotional strategies.

- Focus on High Value Customer Segments

Develop customer loyalty and retention programs for customer segments that contribute the highest revenue to encourage repeat purchases.

- Optimize Product Portfolio

Continuously evaluate product performance by expanding successful product lines and reviewing underperforming products for improvement or replacement.

- Improve Demand Forecasting

Leverage historical sales trends to improve inventory planning and demand forecasting, reducing stock shortages and excess inventory.

- Optimize Shipping Operations

Monitor shipping mode performance to ensure cost effective, timely, and reliable deliveries while maintaining customer satisfaction.

- Establish Continuous Performance Monitoring

Maintain and regularly update the Power BI dashboard to track key performance indicators, monitor trends, and support ongoing data driven decision making.

## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel SQL and Microsoft Power BI can transform raw transactional sales data into actionable business insights. Through data cleaning, SQL analysis, interactive visualizations, and business storytelling, the dashboard provides a comprehensive understanding of sales performance across products, customers, and regions.
