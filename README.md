# GLOBAL-SUPERSTORE-REVENUE-SALES-PERFORMANCE-DASHBOARD

Overview

Businesses generate thousands of transactions daily, but identifying where revenue comes from is often challenging without effective reporting.

This project analyzes four years of Global Superstore sales data (2015–2018) using SQL Server and Power BI to identify the highest-performing product categories, regions, customer segments, and products. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights that support strategic business decisions.

⸻

🎯 Business Problem

Management needs visibility into the key drivers of revenue across products, customers, and geographic regions.

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

Which product categories generate the most revenue?
Which regions contribute the highest sales?
Which customer segments drive business growth?
Which products are the top revenue contributors?
How do sales trends change over time?
Which shipping modes are used most frequently?

🔍 Key Insights

Technology generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
The West Region recorded the highest sales performance.
Consumer customers generated the largest share of revenue.
Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
Revenue showed consistent growth throughout the analysis period.
